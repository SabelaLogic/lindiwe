Writing

She wrote before she cooked.

Short stories for the radio, a long time ago. The same plain voice now sells the jar and the bones.

“Di ordera tsa lona tsa di vaseline le meriri.”
“First come, first serve. Meatybones R69.99.”

The potato

The potato

I cook the potato. I press the water out. The jelly takes what is left.

People say vaseline. I let them say it. The jar has to say what it is.

If you want the pot, you come and sit. I will show you the work. I will not give you my hand.

First come

First come

The bones are not the same story as the jar.

Bones are cold. They go while they are cold. If you are late, the bag is gone.

Five kilograms. R69.99. First come, first served. That is the whole poster.
